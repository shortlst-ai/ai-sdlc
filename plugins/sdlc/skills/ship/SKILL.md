---
description: Ask a person to sign off a work item whose review didn't plainly pass, or a track that always needs it. Use at the merge stage when next.action is approve.
---

Show the person the review findings and the verify result. Never merge it yourself.

Then ask the person to type `/sdlc:approve <name of this item>`, in this session or any other on this repo. This session picks up by itself once they have. If it doesn't, they can use `/sdlc:next`.
